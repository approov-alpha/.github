---

### Collaborating & Contributing
We welcome customer feedback, testing, and collaboration on pre-release software:

1. **Fork the repository:** Click the **Fork** button on the repo you want to test.
2. **Experiment locally:** Build and run tests against your integration scenario.
3. **Open an Issue or PR:** Submit bug reports, feature discussions, or pull requests from your fork back to our repository.
4. **Lifecycle Promotion:** Once projects stabilize, they are transferred directly to [Approov Beta](https://github.com/approov-beta) or [Approov Production](https://github.com/approov).
